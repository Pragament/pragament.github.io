FLEXIBILITY WITH CONSISTENCY

Choose your teaching shift

Monday–Saturday · 9 AM–4 PM IST

Teaching slots are available during school timings. Choose your teaching day(s) and a fixed shift, with a minimum of 2 working hours daily within your chosen shift.

After selection, you must stick with your chosen shift. This consistency helps maintain student alignment and reliable class scheduling.

Before you apply

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews applications and shortlists candidates for technical, mock teaching, and HR rounds. Selected candidates then complete a short training process before the agreement is issued.

What kind of role is this?

This is a contractual teaching role. You’ll sign a short-term agreement with fixed responsibilities, hours, and shift expectations. It is not a freelance engagement or a full-time employment position.

Who will I teach?

Our programs are designed for children aged 6–16 years. Each session is tailored to match their skills and learning level, whether it takes place online, in a school classroom, or in a school lab.
